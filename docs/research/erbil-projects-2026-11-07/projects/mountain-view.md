### 3. English
Mountain View is a residential villa project in the Hawleri Nwe (New Erbil) area of Erbil. Paya Real Estate names GreenPoint as the developer. The project covers an area of 175,750 m2 and consists of 309 villas in two sizes, 240 m2 and 300 m2. Paya lists the project as under construction, and on Homele in November 2026 ready houses were advertised for sale and for rent. Paya shows a 300 m2 house with six bedrooms and five bathrooms at 320,000 US dollars. On Homele, 240 m2 houses with four bedrooms were advertised for sale at 235,000 to 280,000 US dollars, 300 m2 houses at 325,000 to 360,000 US dollars, and a 300 m2 corner house for rent at 1,100 US dollars per month. The payment plan published by Baghy Shaqlawa is linked to construction stages: an advance payment, then payments after completion of the structure, after completion of the water and electricity systems, after completion of the exterior, and on delivery of the house. Services named by both Paya and Baghy Shaqlawa are 24-hour electricity, central gas distribution, a gym, a playground, green spaces and a public garden, a mosque, a school, a supermarket and security.
