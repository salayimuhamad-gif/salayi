### 3. English
West Park Complex is a completed commercial office and retail complex in Erbil, located near Sami Abdulrahman Park opposite English Village, close to Italian Village 1 between the 100 m and 40 m streets. The complex contains 21 offices ranging from 100 to 640 m² and nine commercial shops from 70 to 400 m², and its units are offered for rent only. Office rents are quoted at USD 6 to 8 per square metre, with a service charge of USD 1 per square metre; listed examples include a 108 m² office at USD 648 and a 640 m² office at USD 3,840, and shops from USD 1,400 for 70 m². Features include 24-hour electricity and security, private car parking, central cooling and heating, high-speed internet, a cafeteria, reception and a currency exchange.
