### 3. English
Siraj Towers is a residential apartment project under construction in Erbil. It is located near Ankawa in the Kuran Ankawa area, with easy access to the airport, and is developed by Lazgin Company. The project consists of four towers of 31 floors with a total of 800 apartments. Unit types include 90 m² studios, one-bedroom apartments of 98 m² and 122 m², two-bedroom apartments of 134 m² to 174 m², three-bedroom apartments of 201 m² to 222 m² and larger three-bedroom units of 308 m² to 355 m², and four-bedroom apartments of 253 m² to 268 m². The apartments are designed with floor-to-ceiling windows and open-plan layouts. Units are sold for cash or on instalments, with a payment plan of 20% down payment, 15% on tower completion, 20% on receipt of keys and 45% in monthly payments. In November 2026, the project price was set at USD 850 per m², and off-plan apartments were advertised from USD 79,000 for 90 m² to USD 224,000 for 253 m².
