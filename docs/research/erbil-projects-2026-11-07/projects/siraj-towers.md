### 3. English
Siraj Towers is a residential apartment project under construction in Erbil, developed by Lazgin Company. It is located near Ankawa in the Kuran Ankawa area, with access to Erbil International Airport. The project consists of four towers of 31 floors with a total of 800 apartments. Unit types include studios of 90 m², one-bedroom apartments of 98 m² and 122 m², two-bedroom apartments of 134 m² to 174 m², three-bedroom apartments of 201 m² to 222 m² with larger three-bedroom units of 308 m² to 355 m², and four-bedroom apartments of 253 m² to 268 m². The apartments are designed with floor-to-ceiling windows and open-plan layouts.
