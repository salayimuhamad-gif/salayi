### 3. English
Mass Village is a completed villa project in Erbil developed by Mass Group (MassCity Group), located between the 120 m Street and Masif Salahaddin Road. The project comprises 382 villas on 320 m² plots in three designs: Type A, Type B and Type C. Each villa has a ground floor and a first floor with living areas, a modern kitchen, several bedrooms, private balconies, central heating and double-glazed windows, and is surrounded by a private garden and terrace with a dedicated parking space. Community facilities include 24-hour electricity, security personnel, CCTV surveillance, playgrounds, green areas, a supermarket and a mosque, and pets are allowed. In November 2026, 320 m² villas with four bedrooms were shown on a project page at USD 339,900 to 368,500, market listings offered 320 m² villas for sale at USD 285,000 to 450,000, and villas were offered for rent at USD 1,000 to 1,600 per month.
