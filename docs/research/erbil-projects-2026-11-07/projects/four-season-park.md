### 3. English
Four Season Park is a residential apartment project on the main Erbil–Pirmam (Masif Salahaddin) road in Erbil, with access to the 120 m and 150 m ring roads. It is next to the Hiror restaurant and near Zin City. The project covers 15,270 m² and is under construction. It offers a studio and apartments with one to three bedrooms. The studio measures 58 m² with one bathroom; one-bedroom apartments measure 87, 89 and 90 m² with one bathroom; two-bedroom apartments measure 124 and 134 m² with two bathrooms; and the three-bedroom apartment measures 171 m² with three bathrooms. Planned facilities include a 9,000 m² landscaped garden, underground car parking with direct access to the buildings, security surveillance across the complex, a supermarket, 24-hour maintenance and cleaning, video intercoms, fire-fighting and automatic fire alarm systems, emergency stairs, a smart home system, energy-saving heating and cooling, and a gas system.
