### 3. English
Four Season Park (4 Seasons Park) is a residential apartment project on the Erbil–Pirmam (Masif Salahaddin) road in Erbil, with access to the 120m and 150m ring roads, next to the Hiror restaurant and near Zin City. The project covers 15,270 m², including 9,000 m² of landscaped garden; Paya Real Estate states that green areas make up 60% of the total area and open areas 70%, and that car parking was placed underground to keep the green areas free. Apartments start with a 58 m² studio with one bathroom and include one-bedroom units of 87, 89 and 90 m², two-bedroom units of 124 and 134 m² with two bathrooms, and a three-bedroom unit of 171 m² with three bathrooms. Planned services include water, electricity, drainage, telecommunications and internet, security surveillance across the complex, 24-hour maintenance and cleaning, emergency exit stairs, garbage storage areas, video intercoms, underground parking with direct interior access, fire-fighting and automatic fire alarm systems, leisure parks, an energy-saving heating and cooling system, a smart home system and a gas system. The project is under construction. Prices on the agency project page range from USD 44,536 for the studio to USD 130,644 for the three-bedroom apartment.
