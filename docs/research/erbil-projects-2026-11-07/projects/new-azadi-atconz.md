### 3. English
New Azadi is a residential housing project in Erbil, located on Bahrka Road. A Roya International listing places it near the 120m street on Bahrka Road, gives a total area of 766,150 m2 and describes 648 two-floor houses with gardens. Homele lists the area as New Azadi (Atconz), and Building.co.uk reported that Atconz Real Estate was building a 1,565-home development under the New Azadi name, with 810 two-bedroom houses, 700 four-bedroom houses and 55 five-bedroom houses, together with schools, a hotel and parks. Baghy Shaqlawa lists three house types: 200 m2 plots with a single-floor Type A and a two-floor Type B, two-floor houses on 300 m2 plots, and two-floor houses on 312 m2 plots. Services listed by Baghy Shaqlawa include 24-hour electricity, internet, a playground, preinstalled air conditioners, security, parking, green areas, maintenance and a school. On Homele in November 2026, ready 300 m2 houses with four or five bedrooms were advertised for sale at 327,500 to 412,500 US dollars and a two-floor 312 m2 house at 480,000 US dollars. Houses of 300 m2 were offered for rent at 1,100 to 1,400 US dollars per month, and a 300 m2 office at 2,300 US dollars per month.
