### 3. English
Central Park is a residential apartment project on Gulan Street in the Ronaki (Ronaky) district of Erbil. The project covers a land area of 41,000 m² and consists of 10 buildings with a total of 2,052 apartments. It offers three apartment types. One-bedroom (1+1) apartments of 64 m² have a bedroom, a reception room, a kitchen, a bathroom and a balcony. Two-bedroom (2+1) apartments of 94 to 100 m² have two bedrooms, a reception room, a kitchen, two bathrooms and a balcony. Three-bedroom (3+1) apartments of 156 m² have three bedrooms, a reception room, a kitchen, three bathrooms and a balcony.
