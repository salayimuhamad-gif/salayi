### 3. English
Central Park is a residential apartment project in Erbil, located on Gulan Street in the Ronaki district. The development covers a land area of 41,000 m² and consists of 10 residential towers with a total of 2,052 apartments, all served by a central gas system. Three apartment layouts are offered: 1+1 units of 64 m² with one bedroom, a reception room, kitchen, one bathroom and a balcony; 2+1 units of 94–100 m² with two bedrooms, a reception room, kitchen, two bathrooms and a balcony; and 3+1 units of 156 m² with three bedrooms, a reception room, kitchen, three bathrooms and a balcony. Listed project facilities include covered and open car parking, a gym and a women's gym, a mosque, reception, security, a children's play area, a library, laundry, a barber and a women's salon, and air conditioning. Apartment prices published on the project page range from USD 55,300 for a 64 m² unit to USD 145,300 for a 156 m² unit (USD 78,600 for 94 m² and USD 82,700 for 100 m²).
