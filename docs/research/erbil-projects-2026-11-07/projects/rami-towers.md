### 3. English
Rami Towers is a completed residential project in Erbil, developed by Rami Holding Company. It is located in the Golden Square area next to Italian Village 1, about 750 metres from Sami Abdulrahman Park. The project consists of 10 residential towers of 31 floors each above a commercial podium, with 2,976 units in total. Studio apartments of 70, 76 and 80 m² combine the living space, kitchen and bedroom, with one bathroom and a balcony. One-bedroom apartments of 90, 95, 100, 105 and 115 m² have a living room, a kitchen and a balcony. Two-bedroom apartments of 160, 165 and 170 m² have one en-suite bedroom, a living room, a kitchen, two bathrooms and a balcony. Three-bedroom apartments of 210 and 225 m² have one en-suite bedroom, a living room, a kitchen, three bathrooms and two balconies. Facilities include underground and covered parking, shops in the commercial podium, a supermarket, green areas and a public garden, children's playgrounds, a gym, security and a reception.
