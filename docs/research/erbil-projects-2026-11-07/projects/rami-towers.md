### 3. English
Rami Towers is a completed residential project in Erbil, developed by Rami Holding Company. It is located in the Golden Square area between Italian Village 1 and Roya Tower, about 750 metres from Sami Abdulrahman Park. The project consists of 10 residential towers with a commercial podium, and each tower has 31 floors. Project statistics list 2,976 units. Studio (0+1) apartments of 70, 76 and 80 m² combine the living space, kitchen and bedroom, with one bathroom and a balcony. One-bedroom (1+1) apartments of 90, 95, 100, 105 and 115 m² have a living room, a kitchen, two bathrooms and a balcony. Two-bedroom (2+1) apartments of 160, 165 and 170 m² have one en-suite bedroom, a living room, a kitchen, two bathrooms and a balcony. Three-bedroom (3+1) apartments of 210 and 225 m² have one en-suite bedroom, three bathrooms and two balconies. Prices on the project page range from $75,250 for 70 m² to $230,625 for 225 m², and a price of $750 per square metre is listed on another agency page. Apartments in the towers are advertised for resale and for rent. Facilities include underground and covered parking, a commercial podium, green areas and a public garden, playgrounds, a gym, security and a reception.
