### 3. English
Centro Tower is a single mixed-use tower in Erbil combining residential apartments with business offices. The project is designed on a site of 5,000 m² and contains 172 residential units, with two floors dedicated to business offices and 1,600 m² of garden and green space. Underground parking with direct interior access is provided. Apartments range from studios to two-bedroom units: studios of 47.8, 49.45 and 55.1 m² with one bathroom; one-bedroom apartments of 86, 86.15, 87.5, 88.2, 91.8, 92.6, 92.75 and 102.5 m² with one bathroom; and two-bedroom apartments of 107, 112 and 125 m² with two bathrooms. Listed facilities include car parking, reception, maintenance, a gym, an indoor swimming pool, air conditioning, a public garden and a sewer connection. The tower is completed and units are offered ready for handover. Apartment prices on the project's Paya page range from USD 44,215 for a 47.8 m² studio to USD 115,625 for a 125 m² two-bedroom unit; market listings for the tower include a 107 m² two-bedroom apartment at USD 89,000, an 85 m² one-bedroom at USD 70,000 and a 55 m² studio at USD 36,000.
