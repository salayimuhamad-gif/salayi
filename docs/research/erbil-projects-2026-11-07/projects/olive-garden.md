### 3. English
Olive Garden is a villa project under construction in Erbil, located between the 150 m Road and the green belt. The developer is Redan Group Company. The project offers houses of 200 m², 250 m² (Types A and B) and 320 m² (Types A and B), each with a ground floor, a first floor and private parking. The 200 m² house has four rooms, two kitchens (a cold kitchen and a hot kitchen) and a garden of 50 to 75 m². The 250 m² house has four rooms, two kitchens and a 125 m² garden. The 320 m² house has five rooms, two kitchens, six bathrooms and a 160 m² garden. The plan includes three gardens with an area of 18,000 m², football and tennis grounds, two schools, landscaped green spaces, walking paths, recreational facilities and commercial areas. The Paya agency page lists the houses at USD 177,000 for 200 m², USD 222,000 for 250 m² and USD 304,000 for 320 m². The payment plan starts with a 20% down payment, followed by five payments of 10% every four months from March 2026 to July 2027, 10% at key handover in November 2027, and 20% in monthly payments over 30 months.
