### 3. English
Eskan Towers is a completed residential tower complex on 60 Meter Street in the Eskan area of central Erbil, close to Eskan Street with its food stores, cafeterias and shopping mall. The towers have shops on the ground floor and apartment floors above, with parking floors for cars. The project offers studios of 57 m², one-bedroom apartments of 53 to 60 m² and of 83 and 90 m², two-bedroom apartments of 85, 96, 118 and 136 m², and three-bedroom apartments of 137 m² with three bathrooms. The smaller one-bedroom units each have a bedroom, a reception room, a kitchen, a bathroom and a balcony. The complex has security and car parking.
