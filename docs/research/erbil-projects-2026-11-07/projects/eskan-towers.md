### 3. English
Eskan Towers is a completed residential tower complex on 60 Meter Street in the Eskan area of central Erbil, close to Eskan Street with its food stores, cafeterias and shops. Bagh-y Shaqlawa names the Shaxa Barza and 99 companies as its builders. The towers have commercial units on the ground floor and apartment floors above, with parking floors for cars. The apartment range includes studios of 57 m²; one-bedroom (1+1) units of 53, 55, 56, 57 and 60 m² and of 83 and 90 m², each with a bedroom, a living room, a kitchen, a bathroom and a balcony; two-bedroom (2+1) units of 85, 96, 118 and 136 m²; and three-bedroom (3+1) units of 137 m² with three bathrooms. The complex has security and parking. Apartments are traded on the resale market: advertised units of 63–120 m² are priced at about USD 41,500–61,000, and one- and two-bedroom apartments rent for USD 320–425 per month.
