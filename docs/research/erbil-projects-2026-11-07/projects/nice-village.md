### 3. English
Nice Village is a residential villa project on Pirmam Road in Erbil, about 1 km from the 120m street, 7 km from the city centre and 13 km from the airport. Miran Real Estate names North Light Group as the developer and lists the project as completed, while Paya Real Estate lists it as under construction. According to Miran, the project has 151 villas set within 40,441 m2 of green space, and Paya states that the green areas extend inside and outside the villas. The villas are grouped in neighbourhoods of seven, with post-modern facades using arches and columns. Miran lists a villa type of 561 m2. Each villa has several living areas, separate hot and cold kitchens, four or more bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms, master suites with balconies, a Turkish bath and maid's quarters. On Homele in November 2026, ready villas of 504 to 650 m2 with five bedrooms were advertised for sale at 570,000 to 750,000 US dollars, and an off-plan 500 m2 corner villa was advertised at 500,000 US dollars.
