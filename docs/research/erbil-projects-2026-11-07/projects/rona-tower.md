### 3. English
Rona Tower, also called Rona Towers, is a residential project on Kirkuk Street in Erbil, opposite the 92 Apartments. Construction was started in August 2020 by Kirmanj Construction Company, and the project is completed. It is built on an area of 8,000 m², of which 5,000 m² is green space. The project consists of two towers of 30 floors each. The garage floors provide 650 parking spaces, the ground floor contains shops, and the remaining floors hold 600 apartments of different sizes. Apartment types are one-bedroom (1+1) units of 79 m² and 85.5 m², each with a living room, an open kitchen, a bathroom, a balcony and a laundry area, and two-bedroom (2+1) units of 121 m² and 127.5 m² with a living room, an open kitchen, two bathrooms and a laundry area; the 127.5 m² type has two balconies. Prices on the project page are $60,000 for 79 m², $62,000 for 85.5 m² and $92,000 for 127.5 m². Furnished apartments are advertised for resale at $59,000 for 79 m² and $88,000 for 127 m². Facilities include parking, shops, green spaces, a public garden, playgrounds, a gym, security, a supermarket and kitchen appliances.
