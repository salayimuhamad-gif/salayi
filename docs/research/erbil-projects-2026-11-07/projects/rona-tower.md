### 3. English
Rona Tower, also called Rona Towers, is a completed residential project on Kirkuk Street in Erbil, opposite the 92 Apartments. It is a separate project from Rona Village. Construction by Kirmanj Construction Company started in August 2020. The project stands on a site of 8,000 m², of which 5,000 m² is green space, and consists of two towers of 30 floors each with a total of 600 apartments. The ground floor contains shops, and garage floors provide 650 parking spaces. One-bedroom (1+1) apartments of 79 and 85.5 m² have a living room with an open kitchen, a bathroom, a balcony and a laundry area. Two-bedroom (2+1) apartments of 121 and 127.5 m² have a living room with an open kitchen, two bathrooms and a laundry area; the 127.5 m² type has two balconies. Kitchens come with pre-installed appliances. Facilities include green spaces, a public garden, a children's play area, a gym, security and a supermarket.
