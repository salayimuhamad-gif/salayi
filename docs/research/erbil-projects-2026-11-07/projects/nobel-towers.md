### 3. English
Nobel Towers is a completed residential project in Erbil, located on the Ankawa – New Bahirka road opposite the Lebanese Village, in the Nobel City area. The developer is Domus Company. The project consists of two buildings, A and B, each with 23 floors, of which three floors are for parking and two floors are commercial. Each floor has 12 apartments of different sizes. One-bedroom apartments (1+1) measure 69, 83 and 84 m² and include a bedroom, living room, kitchen, bathroom and balcony. Two-bedroom apartments (2+1) measure 117 and 118 m² and include two bedrooms (one master), a living room, kitchen, two bathrooms and a balcony. The towers overlook the old city of Erbil on one side and Nobel City on the other. Listed features include 24-hour electricity, a gym, a sauna room, a children's play area, private parking, fitted kitchens, installed air conditioners and landscaped gardens. In November 2026, the Paya agency page listed an 81 m² one-bedroom apartment at USD 62,800 and a 117 m² two-bedroom apartment at USD 102,375, and an 83 m² one-bedroom apartment was advertised for rent at USD 300 per month.
