### 3. English
Lalav Airport View, also called Airport View, is a residential apartment project by Lalav Group in Erbil. It is located on 120 Meter Road, opposite Erbil International Airport. The project consists of five 20-storey residential towers containing more than 1,100 apartments, with underground parking for residents and guests. Apartment types range from studios to three-bedroom units: studios of 42 to 44 m², one-bedroom (1+1) apartments of 75 to 80 m², two-bedroom (2+1) apartments of 97 to 149 m², and three-bedroom (3+1) apartments of 150 and 152 m². One-bedroom units include a living room, kitchen and bathroom, two-bedroom units have two bathrooms, and three-bedroom units have three bathrooms. The project is completed, and apartments are offered for sale and for rent. Facilities within the project include shops, restaurants and cafeterias, a supermarket, a swimming pool, an indoor fitness centre, children's play areas, green spaces and a security centre with round-the-clock surveillance. Residents also have 24-hour electricity and internet service.
