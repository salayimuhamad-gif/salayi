### 3. English
Ruby Towers is a completed residential project in Erbil, located near the 40 m Road opposite the Quattro project and next to the Presidency of the Kurdistan Region. The project consists of six towers with 32 residential floors and 1,640 units, together with four basement floors for parking and two floors for commercial services, and 40% of the project is devoted to green space. Apartments have one, two or three bedrooms, including 88 m² and 93 m² units with one bedroom and one bathroom, 127 m² units with two bedrooms and two bathrooms, and 210 m² units with three bedrooms and three bathrooms. Project facilities include 24-hour electricity, security, parking, children's play areas, green areas and a supermarket. In November 2026, the project page on a real-estate agency website listed apartments at USD 82,000 for 88 m², USD 86,500 for 93 m², USD 118,200 for 127 m² and USD 195,000 for 210 m², and market listings offered apartments for rent at USD 350 to 750 per month.
