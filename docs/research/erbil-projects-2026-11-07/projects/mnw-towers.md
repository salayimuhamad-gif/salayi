### 3. English
MNW Towers is a completed residential project in Erbil, located on the 40m Street (Gulan Street) in the Waziran neighbourhood, opposite Dream City and the Park View project, beside Star Towers. The street continues to Sami Abdulrahman Park. The project has four buildings, named B1, B2, B3 and B4, each with 17 floors, and a total of 374 apartments. Apartment types are one-bedroom units of 65 m2 with a reception room, kitchen, bathroom and balcony; two-bedroom units of 110, 115, 120 and 125 m2 with two bathrooms; three-bedroom units of 150, 160 and 185 m2 with three bathrooms; and four-bedroom units of 215 m2 with four bathrooms and three balconies. The project includes shops and a commercial centre, and a sports centre with a swimming pool, sauna and steam bath. Other services named by the agencies are 24-hour electricity, security, parking, gardens, a gym and a supermarket. In 2026 Miran Real Estate showed a 65 m2 one-bedroom apartment for sale at 68,000 US dollars, and rents on Miran and Homele ranged from 425 US dollars per month for a 110 m2 apartment to 2,000 US dollars per month for a furnished 215 m2 apartment. A 76 m2 shop was offered for rent at 1,800 US dollars per month.
