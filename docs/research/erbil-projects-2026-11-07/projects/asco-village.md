### 3. English
ASCO Village is a villa project under construction in the Diamond Zone (Almas) area of north-eastern Erbil, opposite Grand Majidi Mall near the junction with the resort road, between the 120 m and 150 m ring roads. Miran Estate names Aree Group as the developer. Three villa types are offered: Type A of 230 m² with four bedrooms and four bathrooms, Type B of 275 m² with four bedrooms and four bathrooms, and Type C of 394 m² with five bedrooms and four bathrooms. Paya Real Estate lists prices of US$253,000, US$330,000 and US$472,800 respectively, and Miran gives US$1,100 per m² with a plan of five 20% payments: a down payment followed by four payments at six-month intervals. The villas are built with pumice (Bonza) blocks and include central heating, pre-installed air-conditioning ducts, parquet flooring and Moroccan-style ceilings, with an optional solar system and an optional smart-home system. The project has wide, unified villa façades and a pedestrian-friendly layout with landscaped green areas. The facilities listed include a power station, 24-hour security with surveillance cameras, parking, a kindergarten, a school, a children's play area, a sports court, a swimming pool, a pharmacy and a shopping centre.
