### 3. English
ASCO International City, also listed as ASCO International, is an apartment project in the Diamond Zone area of Erbil, on the Massif–Pirmam road opposite Grand Majidi Mall. Miran Estate lists the project as under construction, with five buildings of 18 floors. Apartment layouts are one-bedroom (1+1) units of 93, 94 and 96 m², two-bedroom (2+1) units of 129, 134, 138 and 140 m², three-bedroom (3+1) units of 181, 186 and 191 m², and four-bedroom (4+1) units of 276 m². Miran gives a price of US$830 per m² with a payment plan of 18% down payment, 22% at tower completion, 25% at key handover and 35% in monthly instalments over 60 months. Homele lists ready apartments in the project for sale, including 93 m² units at US$50,000 and 191 m² units at US$120,000 in November 2026, and a 320 m² penthouse for rent at US$1,400 per month. The facilities listed for the project include a swimming pool, a gym, gardens, a playground, a shopping centre, elevators, 24-hour electricity, parking and security, and Miran lists mountain and street views for its apartments.
