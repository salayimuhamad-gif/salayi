### 3. English
English Tower is a residential project inside English Village in Erbil, developed by King Insaat. It consists of two blocks, A and B, with 42 floors: 40 residential floors and 2 commercial floors. Apartments are offered as studios (0+1) and one- to four-bedroom units (1+1 to 4+1). Listed units range from 52 m² to 211 m²: a 52 m² studio with one bathroom; one-bedroom apartments of 83, 87, 96, 101 and 111 m² with one bathroom; two-bedroom apartments of 125–134 m² and 157 m² with two bathrooms; three-bedroom apartments of 166, 186 and 211 m² with two bathrooms; and a four-bedroom apartment of 193 m² with three bathrooms. Paya Real Estate lists a price of USD 1,070 per square metre, with a 40% down payment and the remaining 60% in monthly instalments over 48 months. The King Insaat sales-centre listing on Homele shows a plan of 20% down payment, 20% on completion of the structure, 30% on exterior finishing and the balance over 36 months. The project is under construction, with completion listed for June 2026. Planned facilities include parking, security, a gym, a public garden, a supermarket and a children's play area.
