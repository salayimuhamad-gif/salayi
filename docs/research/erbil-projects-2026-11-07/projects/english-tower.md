### 3. English
English Tower is a residential project inside English Village in Erbil's Golden Zone, developed by King Insaat. English Village lies between 40 Meter and 100 Meter streets near Sami Abdulrahman Park. The project consists of two blocks, A and B, each with 42 floors: 40 residential floors and 2 commercial floors. The project is under construction. It offers studios and apartments with one to four bedrooms, with areas from 52 to 211 m². The studio measures 52 m² with one bathroom. One-bedroom apartments measure 83, 87, 96, 101 and 111 m² with one bathroom; two-bedroom apartments measure 125 to 134 m² and 157 m² with two bathrooms; three-bedroom apartments measure 166, 186 and 211 m² with two bathrooms; and the four-bedroom apartment measures 193 m² with three bathrooms. Planned facilities include covered parking, security, a gym, a public garden, a supermarket and a children's play area.
