### 2. Arabic (العربية)

### 3. English
Lalav Sky View, also called Sky View, is a residential apartment project by Lalav Group in central Erbil, located on Mosul Street opposite Sami Abdulrahman Park. The project is under construction and consists of six residential towers arranged in two sections, A and B, with more than 2,500 apartments. Apartment types include one-bedroom (1+1) units of 78 to 80 m², two-bedroom (2+1) units of 120 to 122 m², three-bedroom (3+1) units of 165 m², and four-bedroom (4+1) units of 239, 288, 295 and 321 m². Listed prices per square metre are $825 for 1+1 units with a Raparin view, $875 for 1+1 units with a garden view, $840 for 2+1 units and $825 for 3+1 units with an outside view. The payment plan is 17% as a first payment, 15% at completion of the structure, 18% on handover and the remaining balance in monthly instalments. Planned facilities include a nursery and kindergarten, a health centre, management and security offices, a supermarket, a gym and fitness centre, a mosque, retail stores, a four-storey car park, landscaped areas and children's playgrounds.
