### 3. English
Lalav Sky View, also called Sky View, is a residential apartment project by Lalav Group in central Erbil, located on Mosul Street opposite Sami Abdulrahman Park. The project is under construction and consists of six residential towers arranged in two sections, with retail units on the ground floor. Apartment types include one-bedroom units of 78 to 80 m², two-bedroom units of 120 to 122 m², three-bedroom units of 165 m² and four-bedroom units of 239, 288, 295 and 321 m². Depending on their position, apartments face either the Raparin side or the garden. Planned facilities include a nursery and kindergarten, a health centre, management and security offices, a supermarket, a gym and fitness centre, a mosque, retail stores, a four-storey car park for owners and guests, landscaped areas and children's playgrounds.
