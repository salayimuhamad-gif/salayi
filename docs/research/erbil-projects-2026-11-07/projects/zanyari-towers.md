### 3. English
Zanyari Towers is a completed residential complex on 60m Street in Erbil, opposite Franso Hariri Stadium. The complex was built by Kirmanj Construction Company and consists of three towers of 25 floors each, containing different types of apartments. The 1+1 apartment has a total area of 85 m² and includes a living room with kitchen, one bedroom and one bathroom. The 2+1 apartment has a total area of 119 m² and includes a living room with kitchen, two bedrooms and a bathroom. The Type-C 3+1 apartment has a total area of 204 m² and includes a living room with kitchen, a bathroom and three bedrooms, one of them a suite. Listed features include 24-hour electricity, internet, a playground, security, parking including an underground car park, green areas, a gym, a supermarket and a pool. Erbil One Tower stands beside the complex on 60m Street. In November 2026, 119 m² two-bedroom apartments were advertised for sale at USD 90,000–110,000, and 85 m² one-bedroom apartments for rent at USD 550–600 per month.
