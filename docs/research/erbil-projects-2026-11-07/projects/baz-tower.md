### 3. English
Erbil Baz Tower, commonly known as Baz Tower, is a completed commercial tower on 100 Meter Street in Erbil. The building has a twisting form with a glass façade and is designed as a mixed-use commercial complex combining office space, retail stores and restaurants. Office units are available in sizes from 54 m² to 156 m², including units of 57, 63, 64, 66, 83, 91, 93, 99, 101, 110, 113, 114, 121 and 152 m². The building provides car parking, a reception and security, and is connected to the sewerage network. Office units are offered for sale.
