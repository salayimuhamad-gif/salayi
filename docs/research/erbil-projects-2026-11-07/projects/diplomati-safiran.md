### 3. English
Diplomati Safiran is a completed residential villa project in the Shorsh / Pirmam Road area of north-east Erbil, on the corridor between the 100-metre and 120-metre ring roads, next to Dar Al-Ziyafa. The project consists of 282 villas and contains no apartment buildings; all units are designated for residential use only and may not be converted to commercial use. Villas are offered in four sizes: 240 m², 300 m², 500 m² and 1,000 m², with four to six bedrooms depending on the size. The compound is gated with 24-hour security, a controlled entrance where delivery motorcycles are not admitted, a centralised gas system, 24-hour electricity, internet, car parking, green areas with a public garden, and a children's playground. Ready villas in the 240 m², 300 m² and 500 m² categories are currently offered on the resale market through Erbil real-estate agencies.
