### 3. English
Kany Kasa is a residential apartment project in Erbil, located between 120 Meter Street and 150 Meter Street on the Bahrka road. The project offers apartments from 59 m² to 170 m²: studios of 59 and 72 m², one-bedroom units of 78 to 90 m², two-bedroom units of 110 to 130 m², and three-bedroom units of 140, 155 and 170 m². Listed unit prices range from $41,300 to $119,000, with an instalment plan of 15% as a first payment, 18% at completion of the structure, 22% on handover and the remaining 45% in monthly payments. Services listed for the project include 24-hour electricity, water and security, a fire suppression system, individual meters and split air-conditioning units for each apartment, thermal wall insulation, parquet flooring and electric cookers. Shared facilities listed for the project include green spaces with a fountain, a kindergarten, a pharmacy, a supermarket, a beauty centre and barber shop, a children's playground, a swimming pool, a car wash, a gymnasium and a medical clinic.
