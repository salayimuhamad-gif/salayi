### 3. English
Kany Kasa is a residential apartment project in Erbil, located between 120 Meter Street and 150 Meter Street on the Bahrka road. The project offers studios of 59 and 72 m², one-bedroom apartments of 78 to 90 m², two-bedroom apartments of 110 to 130 m² and three-bedroom apartments of 140, 155 and 170 m². Planned services include 24-hour electricity, water and security, a fire suppression system, individual meters and split air-conditioning units for every apartment, thermal wall insulation, parquet flooring and electric cookers. Planned shared facilities include green spaces with a fountain, a kindergarten, a pharmacy, a supermarket, a medical clinic, a beauty centre and barber shop, a children's playground, a swimming pool, a car wash and a gym.
