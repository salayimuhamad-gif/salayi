### 2. Arabic (العربية)

### 3. English
The Boulevard is a completed residential development on 40 Meter Street (Gulan Street) in central Erbil, opposite Gulan Tower and the Abu Shahab village. The project covers 53,000 m² and consists of 10 residential towers of 20 floors with about 1,600 apartments, together with commercial spaces that serve residents. Apartment types range from studios to four-bedroom units: studios of 69.5 and 72.5 m² with one bathroom, one-bedroom apartments of 92.66 to 125 m² with one bathroom, two-bedroom apartments of 148 to 172 m² with two bathrooms, three-bedroom apartments of 195 to 363.6 m² with three bathrooms, and four-bedroom apartments of 298.5 m². Facilities include indoor and outdoor swimming pools, gyms, green spaces and public gardens, children's play areas, a sports court, a mosque, a pharmacy, a supermarket, restaurants, a cafeteria, a women's salon, elevators, reception, maintenance services, an electrical generator, security and car parking, including covered parking.
