### 3. English
The Boulevard is a completed residential development with commercial space on 40 Meter Street (Gulan Street) in central Erbil, opposite Gulan Tower and the Abu Shahab village. The project covers 53,000 m² and consists of 10 residential towers of 20 floors with about 1,600 apartments, together with commercial spaces for shopping and daily needs. Apartment types range from studios to four-bedroom units: studios of 69.5 and 72.5 m², one-bedroom apartments of 92.66 to 125 m², two-bedroom apartments of 148 to 168.81 m², three-bedroom apartments of 195 to 235.46 m², four-bedroom apartments of 298.5 m², and three-bedroom duplexes of 235.18, 256.55 and 363.6 m². Residents have access to indoor and outdoor swimming pools, gyms, green spaces and public gardens, children's play areas, sports courts, a mosque, a pharmacy, a supermarket, restaurants, security and car parking. Prices listed on the project's Paya page range from USD 82,358 for a 69.5 m² studio to USD 338,798 for a 298.5 m² four-bedroom apartment and USD 448,276 for a 363.6 m² duplex. On the resale market a 97 m² apartment is advertised at USD 105,000, and monthly rents range from USD 400 for a 72 m² studio to USD 1,500 for a 298 m² apartment.
