### 3. English
MRF Towers is a completed residential complex of 11 apartment towers in Erbil, located between the 40m (Gulan) and 100m streets, between Empire World and the English Village. The complex consists of three groups: MRF 2 Towers with 2 towers and 160 apartments, MRF 4 Towers with 4 towers and 320 apartments, and MRF 5 Towers with 5 towers and 400 apartments, a total of 880 apartments. Each tower has 20 floors with four apartments per floor. All apartments are three-bedroom units of 165 m2 with a living room, kitchen, three bathrooms and a balcony. Baghy Shaqlawa attributes the towers to MRF Group, which also built MRF City in Erbil, and Miran Real Estate lists Lalav Group as the developer. Services described for the towers are 24-hour electricity, water, parking, security and elevators. The Miran page for MRF 4 Towers states a price of 980 US dollars per square metre. On Homele in November 2026, 165 m2 apartments in MRF 4 Towers were advertised for sale at 87,000 to 95,000 US dollars and in MRF 5 Towers at 80,000 US dollars, and monthly rents across the three groups ranged from 400 to 650 US dollars.
