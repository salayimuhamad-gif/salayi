### 3. English
Oakland Villas (Oak Land) is a villa community under construction in Erbil, located on the 150 m Ring Road near the Pirmam Interchange. The developer is Kestrel Company. The project takes its name from the Kurdish oak. It is planned with 459 villas in four sizes: 99 villas of 200 m², 278 villas of 250 m², 66 villas of 320 m² and 16 villas of 600 m². The villas are designed with earthquake-resistant structures, natural travertine stone facades, 3.6 m ceilings and smart-home systems. More than 30% of the project area is planned as green space, including a central park of 10 dunams. Planned services include 24/7 security, a central LPG gas system, schools, mosques and commercial markets. The Paya agency page lists four-bedroom houses of 200 m² from USD 200,000, of 250 m² from USD 245,000 and of 320 m² from USD 330,000 to USD 395,000. In November 2026, market listings advertised 200 m² villas from USD 180,000, 250 m² villas from USD 225,000 and a six-bedroom 600 m² villa at USD 1,200,000 on instalments.
