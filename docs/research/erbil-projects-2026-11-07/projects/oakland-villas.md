### 3. English
Oakland Villas, also known as Oak Land, is a villa community under construction in Erbil, located on the 150 m Ring Road near the Pirmam Interchange. The developer is Kestrel Company. The project is planned with 459 villas in four types and sizes: 99 villas of 200 m², 278 villas of 250 m², 66 villas of 320 m² and 16 villas of 600 m². The 200, 250 and 320 m² villas have four bedrooms. The villas are designed with earthquake-resistant structures, natural travertine stone facades, 3.6 m ceilings and smart-home systems. More than 30% of the project area is planned as green space, including a central park of 10 dunams. Other planned services include 24/7 security, a central LPG gas system, schools, mosques and commercial markets.
