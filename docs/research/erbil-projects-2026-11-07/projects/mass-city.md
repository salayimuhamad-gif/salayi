### 3. English
Mass City is a residential villa complex with commercial spaces in Erbil, developed by Mass Holding Group (Mass Group); the project is completed. It is located on the 120 m Street along Masif Road, on a hilly site of more than one million square metres, where green areas, landscaping and gardens make up more than one third of the land. The project is divided into sections with different villa types. The Mass Diamond section offers villas of 240, 320 and 600 m². The Mass Palace section offers villas of 600 and 1,000 m² in classic and modern styles. The Mass Jumeira section offers 320 m² villas in types T3 to T12. In November 2026, houses advertised on the project page ranged from 240 m² houses with four bedrooms at USD 220,000 to 260,000 to 600 m² houses with five or six bedrooms at USD 815,000 to 875,000, and 240 m² houses were offered for rent at USD 1,000 per month. The developer's payment plan has four equal stages: on signing the contract, on completion of the external structure, on completion of interior works and at key handover. Facilities include green areas and public gardens, commercial spaces, a mosque, a supermarket, a children's play area, security, reception and parking.
