### 3. English
The Atlantic, also known as Atlantic City and marketed as Atlantic American Residential, is a residential project of apartments and villas on the Kasnazan road at the eastern edge of Erbil, near the Majdi Land amusement park. The project is implemented by American Claremont Company on a total area of 360,000 m², of which 60,000 m² is allocated to green space. It comprises 28 apartment buildings, each with eight residential floors and four apartments per floor, giving 32 apartments per building, with car garages next to each building. The villa section has 643 villas in two types, A and B: Type A villas have three bedrooms and two bathrooms, and Type B villas have four bedrooms and an area of 200 m². Villa kitchens are delivered with cabinets, a hood, a cooker and a refrigerator, and apartment kitchens with cabinets and a hood. The apartment buildings have elevators, intercoms, double-paned windows and air-conditioning hookups. Apartments currently offered in buildings 22 and 23 measure 65, 71 and 105 m². The published apartment price range is USD 42,000 to 82,500, with a USD 6,000 down payment, staged payments at structure completion, the start of interior plastering and handover, and monthly instalments of USD 350 over 50 months. Published villa prices range from USD 149,000 to 175,000; Type B villas in Block K are offered with USD 30,000 at contract signing and monthly instalments of USD 1,200 over 50 months.
