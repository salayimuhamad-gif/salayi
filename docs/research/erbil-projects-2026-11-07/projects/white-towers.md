### 3. English
White Towers is a completed residential apartment project in Erbil's Golden Square, between 100 Street and 120 Street, opposite Erbil International Airport and near Empire World. The project stands on a land area of 7,785 m², with buildings covering half of the site, and has a total built-up area of 81,000 m². The towers have 17 floors and contain 576 apartments. The project also includes a 3,800 m² commercial area, 2,000 m² of green areas and 21,000 m² of parking over three floors. Unit types are studios of 67 m² with a sleeping area, an open reception and kitchen, a bathroom and a balcony; one-bedroom apartments of 83, 85 and 88 m² with one bathroom; and two-bedroom apartments of 138 m² with two bathrooms. Facilities include 24-hour electricity with generator backup, central gas distribution, internet, pre-installed air conditioning and kitchens, covered parking, a public garden, a gym, a children's play area, a supermarket, a cafeteria and restaurant, maintenance, reception, security and an on-site management team. The project has sewerage and utility infrastructure, and pets are allowed.
