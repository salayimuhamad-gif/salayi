### 3. English
Star Towers, also known as Star Tower, is a completed residential complex on Gulan Street (40 m Road) in the Golden Square area of Erbil, opposite Dream City and beside Waziran, about 5 km from Erbil International Airport. Construction started in 2019. The complex consists of four towers of 14 floors each above three basement parking floors, with a total of 602 apartments and a commercial ground floor with restaurants and cafeterias. A garden lies between the blocks. Studios with one bathroom measure 40 to 63 m², one-bedroom apartments with one bathroom measure 75 to 107 m², two-bedroom apartments measure 112 to 159 m², and three-bedroom apartments measure 149 to 210 m². Services include 24-hour electricity, central gas, internet, installed air conditioners, 24-hour security guards, car parking and on-site facility management.
