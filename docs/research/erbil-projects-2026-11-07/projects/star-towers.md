### 3. English
Star Towers (also Star Tower) is a completed residential complex on the 40 m Road (Gulan Street) in the Golden Square area of Erbil, opposite Dream City and next to the Waziran district, about 5 km from Erbil International Airport. Construction was started in 2019 by MRF Group. The complex consists of four towers of 14 floors each above three basement floors for parking, with a total of 602 apartments. The ground floor is commercial, with restaurants, cafeterias and other shops, and there is a garden between the blocks. Apartments range from studios of 40 m² to 63 m², one-bedroom units of 75 m² to 107 m² and two-bedroom units of 112 m² to 159 m², to three-bedroom units of 149 m² to 210 m². Services include 24-hour electricity, central gas, internet, installed air conditioners, security guards and car parking. In November 2026, listings showed studios for sale at USD 41,500 for 40 m² and USD 48,000 for 62 m², one-bedroom apartments at USD 55,000 for 75 m² and USD 78,000 for 102 m², and a 118 m² two-bedroom apartment at USD 85,000, while apartments were offered for rent at USD 300 to 600 per month.
