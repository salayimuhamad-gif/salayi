### 3. English
Empire World is a large mixed-use development in western Erbil, located along the 100-metre street near Erbil International Airport. Developed by Falcon Group, the master development covers about 750,000 m² and brings together residential, commercial and business sub-projects, each with its own name. Empire Wings provides apartment buildings on the west and east sides of the site, rising from 9 floors upward, with studios, one- to three-bedroom apartments and four-bedroom duplexes. Royal Apartments, in the south-west, comprise 32 towers with 804 three-bedroom apartments of 175 m² and 274 m², together with a swimming pool, tennis courts, a playground and gardens. Royal Villas, in the south-east corner, comprise 300 villas of 320 m², 340 m² and 400 m², built in three phases. The Empire Business Towers at the centre of the site are five office towers: a 27-floor main tower with a conference hall and business centre, surrounded by four 23-floor towers. The Empire Business Complex, at the northern main gate, has six office buildings of six floors each. Other residential components are the Empire Diamond, Empire Pearl and Empire Square towers. Empire Avenue is a pedestrian retail and leisure district with cafés, restaurants, shops, fountains, an interactive park, a women's centre and underground guest parking.
