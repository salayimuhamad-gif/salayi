### 3. English
British Village is a residential villa community in Erbil developed by Cambridge View. It is located on the 60 Meter Road (Peshmarga Road) with direct access from the 150 Meter Road, between Spanish Village and Ganjan City. The project is designed in a classic English architectural style, with red-brick façades, clay roof tiles and chimneys. Villas come in four types: Type 1 of 231 m², Type 2 of 300 m², Type 3 of 370 m² and Type 4 of 500 m², and the advertised villas include four-bedroom units. The gated community is planned with green landscaping and trees, a central fountain, a 2 km cycling route, schools, cafés and markets. The project is under construction and is sold off-plan, in cash or by instalments. The published price is USD 790 per square metre, with a payment plan of five 20% instalments: a down payment, first, second and third payments, and a final 20% paid monthly. On the Miran page, 231 m² villas are offered at USD 182,000 and USD 187,500 and a 300 m² villa at USD 237,000; on homele, off-plan villas are advertised at USD 190,200 for 231 m², USD 242,000 for 300 m² and USD 292,300 for 370 m².
