### 3. English
Rona Village is a residential villa community in Erbil, developed by Doli Razhan Co. It is located on the edge of Erbil's Green Belt in the 150 Meter Road area, a few minutes from Erbil International Airport. The project is under construction. Rona Village is planned for families, with homes, green open spaces and community services. Two two-storey villa types are offered: Type A of 253 m² and Type B of 330 m², both with four bedrooms. Type A villas are listed on the project pages at $144,000 to $154,000, and Type B at $165,000. The listed price is $570 per square metre. The payment plan consists of a 10% down payment, six payments of 10% every three months, 10% at completion of the structure, 10% at handover and 10% in monthly instalments over 12 months. Planned services include 24-hour security, continuous water and electricity, maintenance, a central LPG gas network and solar energy, together with a mosque, a supermarket, a nursery and kindergarten, children's playgrounds and retail shops.
