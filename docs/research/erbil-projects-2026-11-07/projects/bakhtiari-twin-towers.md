### 3. English
Bakhtiari Twin Towers is a completed residential project in the Bakhtiari area of Erbil. It consists of two residential towers of 31 floors each, built on a site of about 7,000 m², and contains 506 apartments, all with balconies. The ground floor is a multi-purpose level with a hypermarket, pharmacy, barbershop and other services, and the project has four floors of parking, landscaped green space and a children's playground. Shared facilities include a swimming pool, gym, sauna, Turkish bath, a hall and a café, along with a generator for 24-hour electricity, air conditioning, elevators, a fire-fighting system, CCTV and security guards. Apartments range from 55 m² studios to one-bedroom units of 78–81 m², two-bedroom units of 111–188 m² and three-bedroom units of 237 m². Apartments in the towers are offered for sale and for rent on the Erbil market, both for cash and in installments.
