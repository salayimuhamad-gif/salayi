### 3. English
Alaska Towers is a residential project under construction on the 120 m ring road in the 32 Park area of western Erbil. Miran Estate names Lorient Construction Ltd as the developer. The project consists of eight towers of 23 floors each, with two parking floors, one commercial floor and 20 residential floors per tower, and about 1,240 apartments in total. Apartment layouts range from one-bedroom units of 100–110 m² to two-bedroom units of about 167 m² and three-bedroom units of about 267 m²; Miran also lists a 266 m² four-bedroom layout. The parking area covers 60,000 m² and holds up to 1,580 cars. Miran lists a price of US$790 per m² with a payment plan of 35% down payment, 30% at key handover and 35% in monthly instalments over 72 months, and Paya Real Estate lists apartments from US$77,500 for 100.65 m² to US$203,072 for 267.2 m². The facilities listed for the project include a school, a gym, a swimming pool, children's play areas, a pharmacy, a supermarket and retail shops, a restaurant, a laundry, an ATM, elevators, 24-hour security and landscaped green areas covering 65% of the site.
