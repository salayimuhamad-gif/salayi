### 3. English
Parosh Twin Towers is a residential and commercial project in Erbil, located on Kasnazan Road (the Koya Road) opposite Plus Life Apartments. The project is built on an area of 6,500 m² and consists of two towers, each with 14 floors and 102 apartments. Besides the apartments, it includes a commercial centre with offices, sports areas, fitness centres and parking. The Paya agency page lists one-bedroom apartments of 80 to 90 m², two-bedroom apartments of 117 and 125 m² and three-bedroom apartments of 162 and 172 m², with one, two and three bathrooms respectively. Prices range from USD 80,000 for 80 m² to USD 170,000 for 172 m²; the two-bedroom apartments are priced at USD 115,000 and USD 125,000 and the 162 m² apartment at USD 150,000. The project is listed as completed and has security and green areas.
