### 3. English
Var Park is a residential apartment project under construction on Masyaf (Pirmam) Road near Shaways in Erbil, behind Future City. The developer is Golden Pelf Company. The project consists of eight buildings rising up to 40 floors, with a stated built-up area of 33,535.93 m² and a building area of 15,672 m². The site is about 20 minutes from Erbil Citadel and 18 minutes from Erbil International Airport. Unit types include a 44.92 m² studio, one-bedroom apartments of 82.73 and 84.5 m², two-bedroom apartments of 129.36 and 134.86 m² and a three-bedroom apartment of 163.42 m². Shops and a restaurant unit are also offered for sale. Planned facilities include an outdoor swimming pool, gym, children's play area, mosque, supermarket, cafeteria and restaurant, public garden, covered parking, an electrical generator, pre-installed kitchens, maintenance and security. Prices on the project page range from USD 36,385 for the studio to USD 122,565 for the 163.42 m² three-bedroom apartment; a 129 m² two-bedroom apartment is advertised on instalments at USD 91,000.
