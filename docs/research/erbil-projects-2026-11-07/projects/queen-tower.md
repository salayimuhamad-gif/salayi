### 3. English
Queen Tower is a residential project in Erbil, located on Naznaz Street (Daniel Qassab Street) near Gulan Street, opposite the Boulevard project and behind Naz Apartments. The project is completed. It consists of two 36-storey buildings with two separate entrances. On each floor, one building has 11 residential units and the other has 8. The two ground floors form the commercial section, and the basement floors are used for residents' parking. Apartment types are 0+1, 1+1, 2+1, 3+1 and 4+1. Listed sizes include one-bedroom apartments of 77–115 m², two-bedroom apartments of 126–175 m², a three-bedroom apartment of 206 m² and a four-bedroom apartment of 240 m². Prices on the project page range from $80,850 for 77 m² to $252,000 for 240 m². Ready apartments are advertised for sale and for rent, with monthly rents between $400 and $800. Facilities include a supermarket, security, a reception, parking, green areas and a public garden.
