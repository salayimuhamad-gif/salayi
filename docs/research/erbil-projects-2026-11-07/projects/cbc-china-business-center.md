### 3. English
CBC (China Business Center) is a commercial building in Erbil, located on Naznaz Street (Gulan Road) between the Dream City and Boulevard projects. The project is a single six-floor building. The ground and first floors are showroom spaces intended for local and international brands, and floors two to five hold executive offices with a unified modern design, with 12 office units on each office floor. Office units range from 89.84 m² to 360.68 m², with sizes of 89.84, 98.38, 101.86, 135.83, 138.93, 188.98, 224.46, 238.54, 240.01, 265.85, 273.24 and 360.68 m². Planned building services include parking spaces, an electricity generator, storage space, CCTV cameras, security staff, waste disposal and a large display screen on the exterior façade.
