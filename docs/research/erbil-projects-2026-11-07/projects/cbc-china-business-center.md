### 3. English
CBC (China Business Center) is a commercial building project in Erbil, located on Naznaz Street (Gulan Road) between the Dream City and Boulevard developments. The building is organised by use: the ground and first floors are showroom spaces intended for local and international brands, while floors two to five contain executive offices with a unified modern design, with 12 office units on each office floor. Office units are offered in a range of sizes from about 90 m² to about 361 m² (89.84, 98.38, 101.86, 135.83, 138.93, 188.98, 224.46, 238.54, 240.01, 265.85, 273.24 and 360.68 m²). A large advertising screen is mounted on the exterior façade. Listed building services include parking spaces, an electricity generator, storage space, CCTV, security staff and waste disposal. The project is marketed as ongoing, with a stated completion date of 31 January 2027. Office prices listed by the project's sales centre range from USD 84,899 to USD 302,971, with a payment plan of 50% at contract signing, 25% within 90 days and 25% on handover of the keys.
