### 3. English
Park View is a completed residential apartment complex in Erbil, located on Airport Road opposite Dream City, about two minutes' drive from Erbil International Airport. It was developed by Salahaddin Holding. The complex has 12 buildings: 11 have a circular design and one rectangular building is named Dream. Type 1 (buildings A1 and A2) has 17 floors with four 310 m² apartments per floor, each with six bedrooms, a living room, kitchen, three bathrooms and a balcony; the top floor has four duplex apartments of 609 m². Type 2 (buildings B1 to B6) has 17 floors with four 246 m² apartments per floor, each with four bedrooms, three bathrooms and two balconies; the top floor has four duplex apartments of 438 m². Type 3 (buildings C1 to C3) has 18 floors with four 183 m² apartments per floor, each with three bedrooms and three bathrooms. The Dream building has 18 floors with eight apartments per floor of 135 m² and 149 m², each with two bedrooms and two bathrooms. On-site services include 24/7 security, underground parking, supermarkets, clinics, salons and restaurants. In November 2026, apartments were advertised for sale at USD 135,000 for 131 m², USD 220,000 for 183 m², USD 240,000 for 246 m² and USD 330,000 for 310 m²; monthly rents ranged from USD 700 for 149 m² to USD 1,500 for 246 m², and USD 4,000 for a furnished 482 m² apartment.
