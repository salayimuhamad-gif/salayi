### 3. English
North Holland is a residential apartment project under construction in Erbil, located on Mosul Road opposite Dream City. The project consists of six towers of 20 floors each, and about 40% of the project area is allocated to greenery and gardens. Apartment types on the Paya agency page are one-bedroom units of 90 and 96 m², two-bedroom units of 135 and 140 m², and a three-bedroom unit of 210 m², with one, two and three bathrooms respectively. Listed prices range from USD 71,100 for 90 m² to USD 165,900 for 210 m². The payment plan is 17% as a first payment, 23% on completion of the structure, 25% at handover and the remaining 35% in monthly instalments. Planned facilities include gardens and green areas, a children's play area, parking and security. In November 2026, off-plan two-bedroom apartments of 140 m² were advertised at USD 100,000 and USD 110,600.
