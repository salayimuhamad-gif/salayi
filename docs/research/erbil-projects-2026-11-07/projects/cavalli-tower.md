### 3. English
Cavalli Tower is a completed residential project on Gulan Street (40 Meter Street) in central Erbil, developed by Shahan Group. It stands on a site of 4,644 m² and contains 745 apartments. Apartment types range from studios to four-bedroom units: studios of 59 m², one-bedroom apartments of 99 and 108 m², three-bedroom apartments of 172 to 209 m² and four-bedroom apartments of 235 and 238 m². Residents' parking occupies four floors, and two further underground floors serve the commercial spaces; the parking areas are monitored by CCTV. The podium contains retail and food outlets, including a restaurant, a café, a cafeteria, a market, a bakery, a dessert shop, a barber, a laundry, a currency exchange and an ATM. Other services include a sports centre, a women's gym and a women's salon, a children's indoor playground, a kindergarten, a medical centre and a mosque. The building has elevators, air conditioning, a reception, security, maintenance services, an electrical generator and green areas. Units overlook Gulan Mall, the city centre and a park.
