### 3. English
Empire Wings is a residential apartment sub-project of the Empire World development in Erbil. Its buildings stand on the west and east sides of the Empire World site, near the 100-metre street, and rise from 9 floors upward. The project offers a wide range of apartment types: studios of about 63 m² to 67 m², one-bedroom apartments from about 81 m² to 127 m², two-bedroom apartments from about 94 m² to 202 m², three-bedroom apartments from about 214 m² to 231 m², and four-bedroom duplex apartments on two floors from about 377 m² to 412 m². The 63.43 m² studio has a sleeping area, kitchen, bathroom and balcony; one-bedroom units have a bedroom, reception room, kitchen, bathroom and balcony; and the smaller two-bedroom units have two bedrooms, a reception room, a kitchen, two bathrooms and a balcony. Facilities within the project include gardens, parking, a children's play area, security, maintenance services and backup power generators. Apartments are sold for cash, and ready units, including furnished apartments, are offered for sale and rent on the Erbil property market.
