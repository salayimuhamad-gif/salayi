### 3. English
Harsham City 3, also called Harsham 3, is a residential house project on the new Bahrka road in Erbil. According to Haji Salam Real Estate, the project consists of 500 houses, each with an area of 200 m², one and a half floors and three bedrooms. It is a separate phase from Harsham City 1 and Harsham City 2, which both lie on the old Bahrka road. Haji Salam lists the project among the projects of Erbil city.
