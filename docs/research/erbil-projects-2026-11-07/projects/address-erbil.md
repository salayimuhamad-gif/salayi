### 3. English
Address Erbil, also marketed as Adress City, is a villa project in the north of Erbil, near the old Bahrka road in the Gird Jutyar area. The project covers a total area of 289,310.07 m² and comprises 421 villas. Green areas occupy 86,793 m², or 30% of the total area. Four villa types are offered: Type A of 240 m² and Type B of 300 m², both on two floors, and Type C of 450 m² and Type D of 1,335 m², both on three floors. The project is under construction, and Miran Estate lists cash payment for its units. The facilities listed for the project include a kindergarten and nursery, a mosque, a supermarket and shopping centre, a restaurant, a pharmacy, a gym, an indoor swimming pool, a public garden, children's play areas, a cycling track, a power station, an ATM, a currency exchange, a reception, parking, security and a sewerage network.
