### 3. English
Wow Tower is a completed residential and commercial development on 100 Meter Street in Erbil, near the Lebanese French University. The project contains 371 residential apartments, with two basement levels, ground-floor parking and outdoor parking. The ground floor houses a hypermarket, a medical clinic, a kindergarten, restaurants and cafes, and the project also includes a shopping mall, a large fitness centre and adult and children's swimming pools. Unit types include a 60 m² studio; one-bedroom apartments of 90, 100, 110 and 135 m²; two-bedroom apartments of 135, 170 and 180 m²; and three-bedroom apartments of 200 and 230 m². Other facilities include 24-hour electricity, air conditioning, security, a supermarket, a pharmacy, a bakery, an ATM, a currency exchange, a women's gym and salon, a children's play area and landscaped green areas. Prices on the project page range from USD 49,500 for the 60 m² studio to USD 178,250 for a 230 m² three-bedroom apartment. Sale listings include a 90 m² one-bedroom apartment at USD 66,000 and a 180 m² apartment at USD 130,000, and furnished apartments rent from USD 500 per month.
