### 3. English
Wow Tower is a completed residential and commercial development on 100 Meter Street in Erbil, near the Lebanese French University. The project contains 371 apartments and has two basement levels, with underground, ground-floor and outdoor parking. A shopping mall and hypermarket occupy the ground floor. Unit types include a 60 m² studio; one-bedroom apartments of 90, 100, 110 and 135 m²; two-bedroom apartments of 135, 170 and 180 m²; and three-bedroom apartments of 200 and 230 m². The studio and one-bedroom units have one bathroom, two-bedroom units have two bathrooms and three-bedroom units have three bathrooms. Facilities include a fitness centre, a women's gym and salon, adult and youth swimming pools, restaurants and cafés, a kindergarten, a medical clinic, a pharmacy, a bakery, a supermarket, an ATM and currency exchange, a children's play area and landscaped green areas. The buildings provide elevators, a reception, pre-installed air conditioning, 24-hour electricity, a sewer connection and security.
