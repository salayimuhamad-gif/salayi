### 3. English
Avenue Square is a commercial office project by Alcove within the company's Erbil Avenue masterplan on the Bahrka Road in Erbil. The development consists of three office buildings of nine storeys each, with 252 office units in total, and a ground floor given over to shops, cafés and restaurants. Published floor plans cover the ground floor, the first floor, floors 2, 5 and 8, floors 3 and 6, floors 4 and 7, and the ninth floor. The project is under construction and its units are sold off-plan. Offices advertised for sale measure from 93 to 169 m², including units of 96, 97, 118, 123, 134, 139, 156 and 161 m². The price per square metre stated on the project's Miran page is USD 850, with payment in cash. Advertised offices range from USD 79,000 for 93 m² to USD 152,100 for 169 m², and two 139 m² offices are listed at USD 132,000. The project provides security for the buildings.
