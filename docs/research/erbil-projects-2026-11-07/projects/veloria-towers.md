### 3. English
Veloria Towers, marketed as Veloria by Shahan, is a mixed-use development under construction on Ankawa Street in Erbil, developed by Shahan Group. It combines residential apartments, hotel accommodation and commercial space on a land area of 13,079 m², with four underground parking levels and a shared garden. The project consists of three towers with a total of 1,088 units. Tower X (X Quarter) has commercial space from the ground to the fifth floor, hotel accommodation on the sixth to thirteenth floors, apartments on the fourteenth to thirty-first floors and duplex sky villas. Towers Y (Y House) and Z (Z House) each have commercial space on the ground and upper-ground floors, garden villas with terraces on the first and second floors and apartments on the third to thirty-sixth floors. Apartments are offered as one-bedroom units of 95 to 118 m², two-bedroom units of 144 to 169 m² and larger units of 210 to 248 m² with up to three bedrooms. Planned facilities include a swimming pool, gym, playground, restaurant, shopping area, nursery, CCTV, security staff, central heating and double-glazed windows. The payment plan requires 25% down, staged payments of 10%, 15%, 10% and 20% tied to construction and key handover, and the remaining 20% in 24 monthly instalments. Payment plans start at USD 114,400 in Towers Y and Z and USD 128,250 in Tower X. Completion is expected in the second quarter of 2029.
