### 3. English
Empire Pearl Towers is a residential sub-project of the Empire World development in Erbil, developed by BC Group. It is located on the 100-metre road on the western side of Empire World, about 1 km from Erbil International Airport and about 2 km from the centre of Erbil. The project consists of seven towers, each with 23 floors: 21 residential floors and two commercial floors. Each residential floor typically has 12 apartments, while tower P6 has 14 apartments per floor, for a total of 1,806 apartments. Apartment types include studios of 48 m², 49 m² and 54.2 m²; one-bedroom apartments from 94.9 m² to 97 m²; two-bedroom apartments from 126 m² to 129.9 m²; and three-bedroom apartments of 178 m². Apartments include bedrooms, a reception room, a kitchen, bathrooms and a balcony. The towers have three underground parking levels, and facilities include gardens, a gym, security, maintenance services and backup power generators. Apartments are offered for sale and rent on the Erbil property market, including furnished units.
