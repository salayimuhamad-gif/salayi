### 3. English
Aram Garden is a villa project under construction in the Diamond Zone area of Erbil. Miran Estate names Dutch Flower Company as the developer, and Paya Real Estate presents the project as a follow-on to Aram Village 2, built on the same concept. The project comprises 1,124 villas in five types: 253 m² and 286 m² villas on two floors with four bedrooms and three bathrooms, and 360 m², 460 m² and 700 m² villas on three floors with six bedrooms and five or six bathrooms. The larger models add service areas such as a maid's room, a laundry room and storage. Prices listed on Paya in November 2026 are US$275,000 for 253 m², US$325,000 for 286 m², US$450,000 for 360 m², US$650,000 for 460 m² and US$1,200,000 for 700 m². Homele advertises off-plan villas in the project with instalment payment plans. The facilities listed for the project include a mosque, a school, a kindergarten and nursery, a hospital, a pharmacy, a shopping centre, a restaurant, a gym, gardens, children's play areas, a cycling track, a fire station, a power station with electrical generators, parking and security.
