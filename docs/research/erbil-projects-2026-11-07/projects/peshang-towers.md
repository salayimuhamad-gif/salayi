### 3. English
Peshang Towers is a completed residential apartment project in Erbil, located on the 150 m Road near the Korean Village. It was developed by R.V. Company. The project has 14 residential buildings, and its plan also includes a school, a supermarket, a mosque and service buildings. Apartments come in three types. One-bedroom (1+1) apartments of 98 m² have a bedroom, a reception room, a kitchen, a bathroom and a balcony. Two-bedroom (2+1) apartments of 102, 111, 115 and 130 m² have two bedrooms, a reception room, a kitchen and a balcony. Three-bedroom (3+1) apartments of 150 m² have three bedrooms, a reception room, a kitchen, a storeroom, two bathrooms and two balconies. Facilities in the project include a public garden, a children's play area, parking and security, alongside the school, supermarket and mosque.
