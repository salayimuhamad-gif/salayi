### 3. English
Florence Tower is a residential project on 40 Meter Street in Erbil, close to the Quattro Towers complex, implemented under the guarantee of Kayar Group. The project consists of two towers, A and B, and is listed by the marketing agency as completed. Apartments come in a range of layouts: studios of 50, 69 and 72 m²; one-bedroom apartments of 77, 79, 80, 92 and 104 m²; and two-bedroom apartments of 97, 98, 120, 122, 139 and 147 m², each with one or two bathrooms. Kitchens are pre-installed. Facilities listed for the project include car parking, an electrical generator, a reception, security, a gym, a children's play area, a cafeteria, a restaurant, a supermarket, a mosque, a public garden and a sewer connection; pets are permitted. Agency prices for new units ranged from USD 45,500 for a 50 m² studio to USD 124,215 for a 147 m² two-bedroom apartment, while resale listings for the same floor areas were advertised at lower prices, for example USD 75,000 to 77,000 for 122 m² two-bedroom units (as of November 2026).
