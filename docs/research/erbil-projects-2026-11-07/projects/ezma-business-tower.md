### 3. English
Ezma Business Tower is a completed commercial office tower in the Naz Naz area of Erbil. The building rises 13 floors and contains 84 commercial offices, positioned in a part of the city that is still developing, within a few minutes' drive of Erbil International Airport and Sami Abdul Rahman Park. Offices advertised through real-estate agencies range from about 114 m² to 432 m², including units of 128, 160, 168, 231 and 237 m², all offered as open-plan workspace without bedrooms. The tower includes a conference hall, a reception area, a cafeteria, a library, car parking, security and a sewer connection. An electronic access-control system has been installed in the building. Ezma Business Tower is aimed at companies and professional firms seeking office space in Erbil rather than at residential buyers, and it is listed as completed and operational by the agency that markets its units.
