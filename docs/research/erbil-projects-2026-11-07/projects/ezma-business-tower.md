### 3. English
Ezma Business Tower is a completed commercial office tower in the Naz Naz area of Erbil, a few minutes from Erbil International Airport and Sami Abdul Rahman Park. The building has 13 floors and contains 84 commercial offices. Office units range from 114 m² to 432 m², with units of 114, 128, 160, 168, 231, 237 and 432 m², all laid out as open-plan workspace. The tower includes a conference hall, a reception area, a cafeteria, a library, car parking, security and a sewer connection, and the building is equipped with an electronic access-control system.
