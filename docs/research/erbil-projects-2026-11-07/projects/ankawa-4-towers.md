### 3. English
Ankawa 4 Towers, also written Ainkawa 4 Towers, is a completed residential apartment project in the Ankawa (Ainkawa) area of Erbil, at the eastern edge of Ankawa near the Bahrka road. The project was developed by Ashur Ban Company and consists of four towers of 15 floors each. Apartments come in four layouts, each with a reception room and a kitchen: one-bedroom units of 97, 115 and 120 m² with two bathrooms and a balcony; two-bedroom units of 130, 140, 145 and 150 m² with three bathrooms and a balcony; three-bedroom units of 200, 203, 215, 224 and 236 m² with four bathrooms; and four-bedroom units of 247 and 255 m² with five bathrooms and a balcony. Facilities include a security monitoring system, a smart apartment system, 24-hour electricity with a generator, internet, covered parking, gardens and green areas, a sewerage system, a reception, installed air conditioners and pre-installed kitchens. The project also has a church, a supermarket, a cafeteria, a restaurant, a sports complex, a gym, outdoor swimming pools and a children's play area. Ankawa 4 Towers is a separate project from MRF 4 Towers.
