### 3. English
Ankawa 4 Towers, also written Ainkawa 4 Towers, is a completed residential project in the Ankawa (Ainkawa) area north-west of Erbil city centre. Paya Real Estate names Ashur Ban Company as the developer. The project consists of four towers of 15 floors each. Apartments come in four layouts: one-bedroom (1+1) units of 97, 115 and 120 m² with two bathrooms; two-bedroom (2+1) units of 130, 140, 145 and 150 m² with three bathrooms; three-bedroom (3+1) units of 200, 203, 215, 224 and 236 m² with four bathrooms; and four-bedroom (4+1) units of 247 and 255 m² with five bathrooms. Each layout has a reception room and a kitchen, and the one-, two- and four-bedroom layouts have a balcony. The facilities listed for the project are a security monitoring system, a smart apartment system, 24-hour electricity with a generator, internet, gardens, a sewerage system, a reception, installed air conditioners, pre-installed kitchens, a church, a supermarket, a cafeteria, a restaurant, covered and open parking, a sports complex, a gym, a children's play area and swimming pools. Apartments are offered on the resale and rental markets; in November 2026 Homele listed ready apartments of 206 and 237 m² for sale at US$115,000 and US$168,000, and apartments of 203 to 247 m² for rent at US$700 to US$900 per month.
