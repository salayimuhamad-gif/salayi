### 3. English
Sky Towers is a completed residential and commercial project on the 100 m Road (Qazi Muhammad Road) in the north of Erbil, next to the Arjaan by Rotana hotel and near Kebab Yassin. It was developed by Galiawa Group. The towers rise 33 floors above four basement levels, which connect the towers and provide parking. The ground and first floors are commercial, and the 32nd and 33rd floors contain two-storey duplex apartments. The project contains 1,860 residential units. Apartments come in four types: one-bedroom (1+1) units of 89, 100 and 103 m², two-bedroom (2+1) units of 131 to 144 m² with two bathrooms, three-bedroom (3+1) units of 178 and 180 m² with three bathrooms, and four-bedroom (4+1) units of 239 and 259 m². The site has a 4,500 m² green space at the front and a 9,500 m² area at the rear for children's play, as well as Sky Park, with walking paths, sports areas, playgrounds and a fountain. The project has three main entrances. Facilities include basement parking, elevators and a smart entrance, security and CCTV, a VRV mini duct air-conditioning system with control in each room, water treatment and filtration, 24-hour electricity with generators, a gym, a supermarket, a restaurant and café, a beauty salon, a kindergarten and a pharmacy.
