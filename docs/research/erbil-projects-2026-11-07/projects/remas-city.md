### 3. English
Remas City is a residential villa project in Erbil, located on the Erbil–Pirmam (Masif) Road. The project is built on an area of 460,000 m², with open and green spaces making up 79% of the total area. Villa buyers with Iraqi citizenship can officially register the villas in their own name. The master plan includes a services zone with a nursery, a kindergarten, primary and middle schools, a mosque, a health centre, a fire station, a police station, an administration building and a conference hall. Commercial areas include shopping centres, shops, restaurants and cafés, and a central main street is lined with cafés, restaurants and markets. Sports and recreation areas in the plan include a golf course, a water park, an electronic games hall, children's playgrounds, outdoor fitness areas, football fields, fitness halls, swimming pools, and jogging and cycling tracks. The project is listed as completed. The project page lists houses of 500 m² and 688 m² with four bedrooms and five bathrooms, priced at $449,650 and $825,600. Ready houses of 500 m² to 688 m² are advertised for resale at prices from $500,000 to $1,600,000. Facilities listed on the project pages include security, parking, playgrounds, green areas, a public garden, a kindergarten and a supermarket.
