### 3. English
Qaiwan Mirador is a mixed-use project by Qaiwan Group on the main Masif–Salahaddin (Pirmam) Road in Erbil, opposite Grand Majidi Mall. The project is under construction. It comprises eight residential towers of 29 floors each, a VIP tower of 40 floors and 124 villas with areas from 240 to 505 m². The project offers studios, apartments with one to four bedrooms, penthouses, duplexes and villas. Studios measure 63 m² with one bathroom. One-bedroom apartments measure 71 and 73 m²; two-bedroom apartments range from 98 to 246 m²; three-bedroom apartments measure 150, 156 and 160 m²; and four-bedroom apartments measure 201 m². Penthouses range from 152 to 478 m² and duplexes from 184 to 402 m². Villa types include 240, 300, 400 and 500 m². Planned facilities include two commercial floors in each tower with shops and cafés, 24-hour electricity, internet, green spaces with a public garden, playgrounds, underground parking, a gym, a school, a mosque, a supermarket, concierge services and security.
