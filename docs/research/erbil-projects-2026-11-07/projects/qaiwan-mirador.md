### 3. English
Qaiwan Mirador is a mixed-use residential and commercial development in Erbil, built by Qaiwan Group on the main Masif–Salahaddin (Pirmam) Road, opposite Grand Majidi Mall. The project is under construction. It comprises eight residential towers of 29 floors each and a 40-storey VIP tower, together with 124 villas ranging from 240 m² to 505 m². The site covers about 145,000 m², and the project statistics list 1,496 units. Two floors of each tower are allocated to commercial services, including shops and cafés. Apartment types range from studios of 63 m² and one-bedroom units of 71–73 m² to two-, three- and four-bedroom apartments, penthouses of 152–478 m² and duplexes of 184–402 m². Villa types of 240, 300, 400 and 500 m² are listed. The listed price is $800 per square metre. The payment plan consists of an 18% down payment, 22% at tower completion, 25% at key handover and 35% in monthly instalments over 60 months. Planned facilities include 24-hour electricity, internet, green spaces, playgrounds, underground parking, a gym, a school, a mosque, a supermarket and concierge services. Off-plan prices on an agency listing range from $180,000 to $620,000.
