### 3. English
Asuda City is a residential area in Erbil on the Kirkuk Road. The Baghy Shaqlawa real-estate website records the project as an apartment project and lists the following services for it: 24-hour electricity, a children's playground, security, parking, green areas, a public garden, a supermarket and a pets-allowed policy. On the property market, a 200 m² residential plot in Asuda City has been advertised for sale. In May 2023 the Governor of Erbil inspected the project to pave the internal streets and install the sewage network in Asuda City; the works are funded from the governorate's development budget and, according to the governorate's statement, cover about 4,560 metres of sewage lines and the paving of 21 streets over a length of 4,575 metres.
