### 3. English
Tulip Towers is a residential and commercial development under construction in Erbil's Golden Zone, next to Sami Abdulrahman Park on Gulan Street and opposite Dream City. The developer is North Light Group. The project consists of four towers containing a total of 1,243 apartments and penthouses. The towers rise 44 floors, including four basement parking levels, a two-storey commercial podium, typical residential floors, two penthouse floors and a roof-garden social floor. The basement car park holds 1,602 vehicles, and the commercial units occupy the ground and first floors. Unit types include a 95 m² studio, one-bedroom apartments of 104 and 113 m², a two-bedroom apartment of 155 m², a three-bedroom apartment of 230 m², four-bedroom apartments of 240 and 255 m², and duplex units of 97, 103, 158, 194, 197, 236 and 238 m². Planned facilities include a swimming pool, gym, children's play area, supermarket, cafeteria and restaurant, mosque, school, kindergarten, health centre, gardens, central air conditioning, security and covered parking. The quoted price is USD 1,050 per square metre, and payment plans start with a 25% down payment followed by instalments. Prices on the project page range from USD 114,024 for the 95 m² studio to USD 322,378 for a 230 m² three-bedroom apartment.
