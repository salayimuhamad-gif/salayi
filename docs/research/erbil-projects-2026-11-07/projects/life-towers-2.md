### 3. English
Life Towers 2 is a residential and commercial project by Tanj Group in the 32 Park neighbourhood of Erbil, directly on the 120 m road, near Erbil International Airport and Sami Abdulrahman Park. The project is under construction and was designed by Al-Memar Consultant Office. Its vertical section comprises residential towers with 869 apartments in studio (0+1), one-bedroom (1+1), two-bedroom (2+1) and three-bedroom (3+1) layouts, built over a podium and four underground parking floors. The project statistics list three buildings of 27 floors. The horizontal section contains 25 two-storey villas of 210 m². Published units include a 63 m² studio, an 89 m² one-bedroom apartment, a 129 m² two-bedroom apartment and a 195 m² three-bedroom apartment. Price lists in November 2026 show USD 44,730 for the studio, USD 60,520 for the one-bedroom unit, USD 83,850 for the two-bedroom unit and USD 121,875 for the three-bedroom unit. The instalment plan is a 20% down payment, 24% on tower completion, 27% on key handover and 29% in monthly payments. Planned facilities include a hypermarket, commercial centres, smart-home systems, central cooling and heating, 24-hour security, a swimming pool, fitness centres, restaurants, a nursery and landscaped green areas with fountains.
