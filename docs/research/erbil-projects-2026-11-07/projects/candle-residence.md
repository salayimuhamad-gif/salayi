### 3. English
Candle Residence is a residential tower project in the Golden Square area of Erbil, located between Italian Village 1 and the English Village, opposite Sami Abdulrahman Park and next to the Rotana Hotel. The project consists of four residential buildings of 48 floors each, with two commercial floors and four floors of car garages. Apartments range from studios to five-bedroom units: studios of 65 and 68 m² with one bathroom; one-bedroom apartments of 83, 88 and 91 m² with one bathroom; two-bedroom apartments of 139, 140, 145, 147 and 150 m² with two bathrooms; four-bedroom apartments of 267 and 277 m² with four bathrooms; and a five-bedroom apartment of 318 m² with five bathrooms. The project is under construction and units are sold off-plan. Prices listed on the project's Paya page range from USD 71,500 for a 65 m² studio to USD 349,800 for a 318 m² five-bedroom apartment. Market listings for the project include a 148 m² two-bedroom apartment at USD 165,000 and a 65 m² studio at USD 53,000, both negotiable.
