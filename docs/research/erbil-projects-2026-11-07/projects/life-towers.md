### 3. English
Life Towers (King and Queen) is a completed residential project by Tanj Group in the 32 Park area of Erbil, opposite Italian Village 1 on the street linking the 100 m and 120 m roads, close to Erbil International Airport and Sami Abdulrahman Park. The project consists of two towers named King and Queen on a shared podium. Each building has 24 floors, including a basement, three podium floors and 21 upper floors. The two towers contain 400 apartments, and the podium contains 97 apartments together with shops and a market. Apartment layouts are one-bedroom (1+1) units of 80 to 110 m² with one bathroom, two-bedroom (2+1) units of 85 to 170 m² with two bathrooms and a balcony, and three-bedroom (3+1) units of 116 to 265 m² with three bathrooms and a balcony. Facilities include a nursery, a gym, a swimming pool, restaurants and cafeterias, green spaces and gardens, a children's play area, security and parking. In November 2026, market listings advertised two-bedroom apartments of 116 m² for sale at USD 65,000 and apartments for rent at USD 350 to 600 per month.
