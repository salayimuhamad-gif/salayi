### 3. English
Empire Royal is the residential sub-project of the Empire World development in Erbil. It consists of two parts: Royal Apartments in the south-west of the Empire project and Royal Villas in its south-east corner. Miran Estate lists B.C. Group as the developer. Royal Apartments comprise 32 apartment buildings. The apartments are three-bedroom (3+1) units: 175 m² units in phases A and B and 273–274 m² units in phase C; each has a living room, a kitchen and three bathrooms. The 175 m² units have one balcony and the phase C units have two. A 350 m² three-bedroom type is also listed. The apartment area includes a swimming pool, tennis courts, a playground and gardens. Royal Villas comprise 300 two-storey villas of 320 m² (four or five bedrooms, phase 1), 400 m² (phase 2) and 340 m² (phase 3). The project is completed, and its apartments and villas are offered for sale and rent on the market, with apartments of 170–273 m² and villas of 320–380 m² advertised.
