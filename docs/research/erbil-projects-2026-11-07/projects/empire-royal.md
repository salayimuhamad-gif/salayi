### 3. English
Empire Royal is the residential sub-project of the Empire World development in Erbil. It consists of two parts: Royal Apartments in the south-west of Empire World and Royal Villas in its south-east corner. The project is completed. Royal Apartments comprise 32 apartment buildings. The apartments are three-bedroom units with a living room, a kitchen and three bathrooms: 175 m² units with one balcony in phases A and B, and 273–274 m² units with two balconies in phase C. A 350 m² three-bedroom type is also part of the project. The apartment area includes a swimming pool, tennis courts, a playground and gardens. Royal Villas comprise 300 two-storey villas in three phases: 320 m² villas with four or five bedrooms in phase 1, 400 m² villas with four bedrooms in phase 2 and 340 m² villas with four bedrooms in phase 3.
