### 3. English
Beverly Hills Erbil is a residential villa project by North Light Group in the Golden Square area of Erbil, on the 120-metre road directly opposite Erbil International Airport. The gated community comprises 386 villas in five types on plot areas of 270 m² (Type A), 320 m² (Type B), 485 m² (Type C), 650 m² (Type D) and 1,050 m² (Type E); a 308 m² villa type is also offered. The project is under construction and is sold off-plan, in cash or by instalments. The published price is USD 1,500 per square metre, with a payment plan of five equal instalments of 20%: a down payment, first, second and third payments, and a final payment on receipt of the keys. A 308 m² four-bedroom villa is offered at USD 550,000. Advertised off-plan villas range from USD 405,000 for a 270 m² four-bedroom villa to USD 2,141,419 for a 1,089 m² six-bedroom villa. The development is planned with a gym, car parking, a kindergarten, children's play areas, gardens and shopping facilities.
