### 3. English
Quartier Français is a residential and commercial development in Erbil, implemented by 99 Group of Companies on the Bahrka Road near Azadi Nwe (New Azadi). The project is under construction, and its completion date is listed as 1 January 2030. It consists of 11 towers designed in a classical French style combined with the urban character of Erbil. Each tower has 36 floors: three basement floors for parking, two commercial floors and 31 residential floors. The project contains about 4,000 residential units, and its area is listed as 60,000 m². About 60% of the project is allocated to green space. Plans include a central plaza inspired by the Louvre, an on-site school and 24-hour security. Apartment types are 90 m² and 98 m² with one bedroom, 140 m² and 158 m² with two bedrooms, 185 m² with three bedrooms and 280 m² with four bedrooms. Listed prices range from $71,100 for 90 m² to $215,600 for 280 m². For the 90 m² apartment, the payment plan starts with a 10% down payment of $7,100, followed by $250 monthly for 36 months, $15,600 at key handover, $350 monthly for seven years and $1,000 yearly for ten years.
