### 3. English
Quartier Français is a mixed-use residential and commercial project in Erbil, developed by 99 Group of Companies on the Bahrka Road near Azadi Nwe (New Azadi). The project is under construction, with completion planned for January 2030. It consists of 11 towers in a French-inspired design and contains about 4,000 residential units. Each tower has 36 floors: three basement floors for parking, two commercial floors and 31 residential floors. Planned features include green space covering about 60% of the site, a central plaza inspired by the Louvre, an on-site school and 24-hour security. The project offers one-bedroom apartments of 90 and 98 m², two-bedroom apartments of 140 and 158 m², three-bedroom apartments of 185 m² and four-bedroom apartments of 280 m².
